What this claim covers

My family and I were passing through Florida for a short stay when the air conditioning failed. The vehicle went to Universal Hyundai on July 7 for what was described as a few days of work. It was not returned on time. Over the next two-plus weeks I was told on several occasions that the car was ready or being worked on when it was not, which left us stranded out of state with a minor child and forced the costs below.

Throughout, I did everything I reasonably could to keep these costs down, not run them up:

  • ⛽Fuel driving back and forth to the dealership. Each time they told me to come in, I drove to the shop in person, often after calls and texts went unanswered. Much of the fuel below is those repeated trips, plus the drive home once it was clear the car would not be ready.
  • 🏨Moved to cheaper lodging. As the stay dragged on, I relocated to less expensive hotels to lower the nightly cost rather than stay put and pay more.
  • 🛒Bought groceries to eat in. I shopped for food and supplies so we could prepare meals and eat out less, cutting restaurant spending during a stay that was never supposed to happen.
  • 🚗Drove the family home. When the car still was not fixed, I drove us back to New York instead of continuing to pay to wait in Florida.
